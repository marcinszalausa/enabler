# Simple paid plans and Try mode

The public choices are Starter $29/month (300 credits), Plus $49/month (600) and Growth $79/month (1,000). Plus is Recommended, not Most popular. There is no introductory, crossed-out, annual or higher renewal price.

Each paid plan advertises one included social account. Extra connections require separately confirmed pricing and paid activation. No unapproved $9 add-on price is published. Legacy ACCOUNT_LIMITS and existing customers are unchanged. Paid checkout remains closed: future activation must implement the new one-included-account/add-on entitlement contract instead of treating the legacy connection caps as new paid allowances. No charge or purchase of account capacity is enabled by this change.

All Try mode buttons use the existing verified-signup, one-time 100-credit allowance. They do not grant the selected plan, refill credits, start a subscription, unlock paid tools or add a new timed trial. The limited creation flow previews the core generator. Monthly allowances and paid tools require paid activation, stated before signup. Existing free and paid accounts retain their credits, permissions and limits.

The selected plan travels as a validated URL/form preference through product entry, login, email callback, setup and product redirect. It never controls authorization, cost, credit grants or workspace.plan. It is not added to the product brief or model context. Choosing a different plan does not create another allowance.

Plus now includes the existing comparison/CSV workflow for up to 16 recent posts; Growth keeps 30, Starter and legacy Free keep eight. These are server-selected limits based on the actual workspace plan. Other existing creation and publishing capabilities stay shared.

The full feature catalogue and FAQ/JSON-LD describe the three paid plans and limited Try mode consistently. The calculator retains a separate 100-credit Try mode example. No provider calls, live posts, account changes or customer-credit spending are used in tests.
