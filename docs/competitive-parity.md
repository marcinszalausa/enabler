# Gooidy competitive parity programme

Target: a credible product-ad creation and publishing workflow for US small businesses, with trustworthy public information, useful editing, clear usage pricing and repeat-campaign value. Navigation similarity is not functional or creative-quality parity.

## Acceptance tracker

- [ ] Discovery: dedicated industry and platform guides, genuine approved examples, searchable help and original educational articles; mobile, desktop, RTL, metadata and link checks pass.
- [ ] Creative presets: reusable, previewable campaign starting points applied to real generation direction without automatic spending.
- [ ] Individual revisions: revise one selected image/video with the exact cost shown, workspace authorization, idempotent charge/refund, recovery and quality checks; keep original assets and unrelated ads intact.
- [ ] Billing: server-defined products, subscriptions, top-ups, customer portal, signed webhook processing, replay-safe credit grants and tested lifecycle behavior. Keep payments gated until Stripe configuration and operator information are complete.
- [ ] Paid promotion: advertising-account connection, explicit budgets, review-before-launch, supported network integration and reporting. Do not confuse organic posting with paid advertising or invent store-sales attribution.
- [ ] Evidence: real customer permissions and measured case studies; a representative provider-backed creative benchmark against comparable inputs and formats.

## Constraints

Keep benefit-led language, fast public pages and face-safe full-frame media. Show only operator-approved showcase assets, with working cached media; use clearly labelled concepts when a category lacks public examples. Do not invent customers, testimonials, ratings, partnerships, performance lifts or support promises. Existing billing prices are not competitor credit units; no fabricated discount or numerical credit comparison.

No live generation, customer-credit spending, email sending, social posting, ad spend or payment charge in automated tests. No public pre-launch banners or invented LLC/operator details. Legal operator fields remain unresolved internally until the owner supplies them. Record deployment and validation per stage. External configuration and real evidence remain explicit blockers; never label them completed because a page or mocked test exists.
