# Clearer reasons to upgrade

Prices and allowances stay Free $0 / 100 once, Starter $29 / 300 monthly and Growth $79 / 1,000 monthly. Account caps remain 1 / 3 / 10. All existing generation quality choices, brand memory, copy edits, credit-priced revisions, downloads, campaign plans, manual scheduling, queue/autopilot settings and basic results remain available as before.

Starter and legacy Plus add a new read-only posting planner, also included in Growth. An authenticated workspace editor selects up to seven different saved ads; the server checks the workspace plan, ownership, successful generation, saved time zone and posting routine. It previews available slots and can download calendar review reminders. It does not reserve slots, schedule posts, spend credits or call the social provider. Each post must still be reviewed and scheduled through existing publishing controls.

Growth adds up to 30 recent published/partial posts to the existing explicit-load results page. Other plans retain eight. Only workspace-owned post IDs reach the provider. Requests are bounded to six concurrent lookups; no analytics prefetch is enabled. The report compares posts within the same network or network combination and exports CSV. Missing data remains missing, captions are spreadsheet-formula escaped, and no sales attribution or winning-ad guarantee is claimed.

Pricing cards lead with customer outcomes and incremental benefits. Shared essentials appear once. Growth's approximately 18% lower price per included credit is calculated from $79/1,000 versus $29/300, not a fabricated discount. Image capacities are an alternative use of the shared allowance. Paid buttons explore benefits; checkout remains behind the existing billing gate.

Validation: pure entitlement/comparison/export tests; actual planner action with mocked authenticated/RLS data and denied free/viewer/foreign selections; responsive pricing and calculator checks; planner controls and actual report downloads. No live generation, customer credit spending, account-plan changes, posting or payment calls in tests. No schema or production-data changes are required.
