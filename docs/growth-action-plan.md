# Growth action plan and Plus reports

Plus and Growth can filter the existing results comparison by social network or network combination. CSV export follows the selected view; missing metrics remain blank and spreadsheet-formula protection remains in place. A badge marks the highest observed click rate in a comparable group, without claiming a controlled winner.

Growth alone adds an exploratory next-test action plan and plain-text campaign brief. The server derives access from the authenticated workspace's stored plan. Query parameters and client-selected signup preferences do not grant access. Existing Free, Starter and Plus access is unchanged.

The plan uses the existing, already-requested results snapshot (up to 30 posts). There are no extra provider requests, generation costs, model calls, mutations, credits, scheduled posts or automatic publishing. The user must review and explicitly create any new ad at its displayed credit price.

An idea needs two distinct ready posts from exactly the same network or network combination. Both must have at least 500 impressions, nonnegative integral click counts no greater than impressions, and different observed click rates. Missing, delayed, malformed, tiny or tied data produces no recommendation. At most three groups are shown, prioritizing greater combined reported reach. Dates, audiences and reporting windows may differ: these are hypotheses for new tests, not causal attribution, controlled A/B tests, statistically proven winners or revenue guarantees.

The download retains source IDs, captions, posting dates, observed rates and caveats. It is a text file, not an executable document. The plan is not durable storage; it is rebuilt from the current results snapshot and can be downloaded.

The public Growth card advertises the unique tool and brief. Plus advertises filtering and observed comparisons. Growth's lower price per included credit is verified against the actual catalogue. Generation quality remains available across plans. Checkout remains disabled.
