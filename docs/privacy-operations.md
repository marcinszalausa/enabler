# Pre-launch manual privacy operations
Public requests go to hello@gooidy.com. No email is automatically sent by a website request link. The owner must verify delivery and assign a person to monitor this inbox before paid launch.

1. Log receipt, request category, account/workspace scope, requester's state when relevant, responsible handler and the applicable deadline. Determine legal applicability with counsel; do not assume one nationwide deadline.
2. Verify through the account email or a verified session, proportionate to risk. Never request login codes/passwords or full card details. Verify agents' authority. Do not disclose whether another person has an account.
3. For access/export, inventory the requester's account, shared-workspace membership, products, creatives, credit events, favorites, connections, posts and support records. Exclude other members' personal data and secrets. Deliver through an authenticated or suitably protected expiring channel.
4. Before deletion, identify shared-workspace ownership and prevent deleting other members' data. Confirm irreversible scope. Handle scheduled posts and provider account disconnection deliberately. Revoke sessions before deleting an auth user; existing JWT expiry must be considered.
5. Coordinate active database rows, storage objects, public showcase snapshots, Zernio media/posts and relevant generation/email providers. Remove affected showcase assets from display and deployment where required. Cached/downloaded public copies may persist; explain this accurately.
6. Apply legal-hold, fraud, tax and dispute exceptions only where justified. Minimize and restrict retained records. Document provider backup expiry and restoration handling. Do not promise a universal 30-day purge before it is tested.
7. Respond within the applicable legal deadline. Document permitted extensions, reasons for denial and appeal instructions. Record the review and outcome of an appeal and any regulator contact required by applicable law.
8. Maintain a minimal restricted request log, avoid raw sensitive content, and establish a retention/deletion period for the log itself.

Before payment activation, run end-to-end export/deletion on a designated fixture, including the storage and provider lifecycle. Record evidence and owner signoff. The policy links do not mean this manual process has been performed for real accounts.
